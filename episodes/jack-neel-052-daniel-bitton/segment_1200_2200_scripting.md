**Move 6 is the one that carries.** He is not describing a writing style. He is describing
a control regime for a generative system: narrow the window, steer each unit, regenerate in
pockets, and never hand the model a block large enough for it to do its own thing. The
reason he gives is the operative sentence — *it's not going to have as much space to mess up.*
